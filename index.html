Before you download

Is OraKit free?

You can download OraKit for free. The free version includes ads; some advanced tools require Pro or an available rewarded video unlock. Check the app for current access options and prices.

Does everything work offline?

Many document tools work on your device. Features that retrieve web content, iCloud services, advertising and App Store purchases require a connection.

What kind of signature can I add?

You can draw a signature and place it on a PDF. This is not a qualified digital signature service.

Can every PDF be reduced to any size?

No. The result depends on the document and its contents. Compression can affect quality; inspect your PDF before sending it.
